## Facts worth not rediscovering

* the game's clock runs ~3 in-game minutes per 10 real seconds - never use it as a timer;
* `probe nativefn` lies; use `observe_fn`'s slot or an actual call;
* `GetCharacterOre` / `GetPawn` / `SetSkeletalMesh` are not UFUNCTIONs; read the properties (`Pawn`, `SkeletalMesh`)
  or use `SetSkeletalMeshAsset`;
* the hero's mesh is a Mutable-generated asset in `/Engine/Transient` - no path; creature meshes are real assets
  under `/Game/Assets/Characters/Creatures/`;
* a transformation **possesses a different actor**; the mod must follow the controller's `Pawn`;
* the pose module keeps its own bone list for the receiver (`R`) - it must come from the puppet, not from the local
  hero, or the other player's animation freezes after a transformation;
* the pose module draws the puppet with a `PoseableMeshComponent` it creates itself - the mesh swap has to reach it
  (it is recreated when the puppet's asset changes).
